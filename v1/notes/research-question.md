# V1 Research Question

Can environmental and remote-sensing variables predict
where and when supplementary irrigation will be needed
before visible crop stress develops in the Northern Jordan Valley?

## Decision output

For each spatial unit and forecast date:

- irrigation needed? yes/no
- estimated irrigation deficit
- urgency
- forecast horizon
- confidence

## Initial forecast horizon

7–14 days

## Scientific principle

The target must represent a crop-water decision,
not merely low soil moisture.

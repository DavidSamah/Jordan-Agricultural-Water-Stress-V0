# H2 — Multi-sensor agricultural water-stress prediction

## Background

Jordan already has:
- drought monitoring research
- SPI/SPEI drought forecasting
- ARIMA/TBATS forecasting
- remote-sensing drought indicators
- MENAdrought monitoring and early-warning systems

## Candidate gap

Has research in Jordan combined:

1. multi-sensor remote sensing
2. soil moisture
3. vegetation condition
4. temperature
5. precipitation
6. evapotranspiration
7. machine learning

to PREDICT agricultural water stress rather than
only describe or classify meteorological drought?

## Stronger validation question

Are predictions validated against:
- crop stress
- crop yield
- irrigation demand
- field soil moisture
- measured evapotranspiration
- agricultural losses

rather than only SPI/SPEI?

# Study Area

## Region
Northern Jordan Valley

## Initial analysis period
2018-01-01 to 2024-12-31

## Reason for region selection

- irrigated agriculture
- water-management relevance
- existing remote-sensing research
- WaPOR applicability
- suitable setting for agricultural water-stress research

## Candidate question

Can multi-sensor environmental observations predict
agricultural water stress in the Northern Jordan Valley
before conventional monitoring indicators show severe stress?

## Status

Study area selected provisionally.
Exact geographic boundary still needs to be defined.

# Research Question

## Provisional gap

Existing Jordan research covers:
- agricultural drought monitoring
- remote-sensing image fusion
- evapotranspiration estimation
- irrigation-water accounting
- statistical drought forecasting
- ML-assisted drought indicator ranking

We have not yet identified a Jordan-specific study combining
multi-sensor remote sensing with machine learning for short-term
prediction of agricultural water stress or irrigation need,
validated against direct agricultural or physical outcomes.

## Primary research question

Can multi-sensor remote-sensing variables be combined with
machine learning to predict short-term agricultural water stress
in Jordan earlier than conventional drought indicators alone?

## Candidate predictors

- soil moisture
- vegetation condition / NDVI
- land-surface temperature
- precipitation
- evapotranspiration

## Candidate targets

- agricultural water stress
- irrigation need

## Candidate validation data

- field soil moisture
- crop yield
- irrigation records
- measured evapotranspiration
- field crop-stress observations

## Status

PROVISIONAL.
The exact target and study region still need to be selected.

# H3 — Predictive value of multi-sensor remote sensing

Hypothesis:

Combining multiple remote-sensing variables will improve
short-term prediction of agricultural water stress compared
with a simple single-indicator baseline.

## Falsification conditions

H3 is weakened if:

- a simple indicator performs equally well
- multi-sensor data add no predictive value
- satellite observations arrive too late for useful warning
- predictions disagree strongly with ground observations
- performance fails to generalize across seasons or locations
